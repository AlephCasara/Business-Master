## 1. How to interpret the matrix

The project is not choosing one permanent business.

The useful research question was:

> Which mix of experiments might combine early cash, fast information, reusable infrastructure and long-term asset formation?

The matrix originally grouped candidates into signal, cash and asset roles. PR9 has since implemented first-class portfolio roles and Capital Control; those implemented semantics outrank this research shorthand.

### Signal-like candidates

Examples considered:
- short-form content;
- YouTube formats;
- product creatives;
- offer pages;
- outbound messages.

### Cash-like candidates

Examples considered:
- productized B2B;
- AI video service;
- lead generation;
- affiliate commerce where eligible.

### Asset-like candidates

Examples considered:
- vertical SaaS;
- owned offer/product;
- channel/IP portfolio;
- proprietary datasets;
- durable supplier/customer network.

## 2. Historical bootstrap snapshot

The original research phase proposed a local zero-incremental-cash mix:

```text
Machine A — Signal Engine
  1 content hypothesis at a time
  small number of channels/accounts
  high measurement density

Machine B — Cash Engine
  1 productized B2B outcome
  automated prospect research + outreach + demo/delivery

Machine C — Commerce Probe
  product/affiliate/marketplace research
  publish only after eligibility and unit-economics gates

Machine D — Asset Builder
  dormant until repeated evidence justifies software/data/product asset
```

This snapshot is retained for research history. It is **superseded as current sequencing** by the low-human-touch bootstrap and PR11–PR14 path in `ECONOMIC_THESIS.md` / `ROADMAP.md`. The old zero-incremental-cash assumption is also superseded by expected-value + PR9 Capital Control policy.

## 3. Method-specific research conclusions

### AI coaching / concierge

**Research signal:** AI expertise can be monetized quickly.

**Constraint:** traditional one-to-one delivery conflicts with the project's human-scarcity objective.

Potential uses:
- paid discovery;
- high-ticket exception;
- pain mining;
- transition into standardized delivery.

Do not make calls the core scaling unit.

### Productized B2B

**Research signal:** strong candidate for early cash + automation in the supplied material, but not current mandatory first sequencing.

Good offers are narrow outcomes, for example:
- respond to every review and summarize recurring complaints;
- repurpose long recordings into a fixed number of shorts;
- generate/schedule a specific content package;
- automate a narrow lead/reply workflow.

The product is the delivered outcome, not "AI automation consulting."

### AI video / UGC service

**Research signal:** media generation can be shared with broader Creative/Production capabilities.

Synergy:

```text
internal media R&D
→ content experiments
→ external client capability
→ commerce creatives
```

### Faceless / dark channels

**Research signal:** automate production/publish/analytics aggressively without locking creative strategy before evidence.

Avoid repetitive AI-sludge patterns. Original usefulness and differentiation remain economic constraints even when platform policy permits AI assistance.

### TikTok Shop

**Research signal:** potentially fast signal and direct commerce attribution.

**Constraint:** eligibility, identity verification, posting caps and geography are first-class account state.

Do not build economics around an imaginary unlimited-account surface.

### Digital products

**Research signal:** generic information is heavily commoditized; outcome-oriented products, tools, templates, proof/support and narrow domain assets can remain viable.

Ask:

```text
What does the customer obtain that a generic model response does not give them?
```

### KDP

Production parallelizes well, but slow validation/distribution uncertainty made it a weak initial cash hypothesis in the reviewed material.

### Ecommerce

AI reduces store/creative/ops work, but does not create demand.

Useful loop:

```text
demand evidence
→ exact product/offer identity
→ contribution underwriting
→ supply/fulfillment reliability
→ creative acquisition test
→ order/settlement evidence
```

### Vertical micro-SaaS

Long-term asset hypothesis; software remains downstream of problem validation.

```text
pain observed
→ customer pays for outcome
→ workflow repeats
→ delivery standardizes
→ software reduces marginal cost
```

## 4. Kill-condition research priors

Potential reasons to pause/kill include:

- no meaningful external signal after the planned evidence window;
- economics cannot become positive under plausible sensitivity ranges;
- recurring human time grows linearly with scale and no productization path exists;
- platform/account eligibility makes the proposed scale structurally impossible;
- fulfillment/reliability risk destroys expected value;
- production cost exceeds expected economic value including information/option value;
- the experiment changes too many major variables to teach why it won/lost;
- the same hypothesis repeats without material mutation.

Implemented family policies/experiment contracts remain authoritative over these research priors.

---

## 5. Graduation research priors

Candidate evidence can include:
- repeated positive contribution margin;
- repeated high cohort-relative content signal;
- multiple independent paying customers for the same productized outcome;
- repeat purchase / retention;
- consistent qualified replies from the same offer/segment;
- stable product/supplier economics across more than one order;
- lower marginal human minutes as volume increases.

One viral video, one lucky sale or one friendly client is a PROBE result, not mature economic validation. Actual graduation authority lives in implemented contracts/family evaluation/portfolio policy, not this matrix.
