# Source Catalog — Business and Technical Material Audited

This catalog preserves the research surface that informed Business Master. It exists so future agents know **which claims came from creator material, which conclusions survived cross-comparison, and which facts still require current verification**.

Creator videos are inputs/hypotheses, not authoritative platform documentation.

Status vocabulary:
- **CONFIRMS** — materially supports an existing architectural conclusion;
- **MODIFIES** — changes or qualifies an earlier conclusion;
- **REJECTS / DOWNGRADES** — claim/method was overstated or poor fit;
- **AUTOMATES** — introduces a reusable technical module;
- **CHEAPENS** — reduces cost/compute/human effort;
- **SYNERGY** — capability benefits multiple engines.

## A. Direct response, ecommerce and funnels

### Bia Feldman — Facebook Ads scaling / Bid Cap material

Material audited:
- `COMO ESCALAR R$101.532,60 POR DIA AS SUAS CAMPANHAS NO FACEBOOK ADS (NA PRÁTICA)`
- `Como faturei R$ 100 Mil em 5 Dias com UMA Campanha (BIDCAP)`

Durable learning:
- campaign mechanics alone do not create the economics;
- offer + funnel + creative angles + continuous creative testing matter;
- backend monetization/upsells can materially alter allowable CAC;
- creative angles saturate and must be replenished;
- Bid Cap/CBO/budget recipes are transient parameters, not architecture.

Impact: **CONFIRMS / MODIFIES** Commerce Engine; requires CreativeController, funnel attribution and LTV rather than a universal ad recipe.

### Gabi Cervantes — Low Ticket automated funnel

Material:
- `Como vender Low Ticket: Funil Automático que vende todos os dias`

Durable learning:
- low ticket can be customer acquisition;
- order bump, upsell, downsell, abandoned-checkout recovery and future offers matter;
- product-market alignment dominates cheap production.

Impact: **CONFIRMS** full-funnel economics and separate AOV/LTV objects.

### Rapid low-ticket / product-test material (Pedro, as discussed)

Durable learning:
- test multiple offer/product hypotheses rather than polishing one unvalidated product;
- use smallest viable sales surface in PROBE;
- deepen VSL/page/brand only when evidence justifies it;
- creative testing velocity often matters before sophisticated funnel optimization.

Impact: **CONFIRMS** Probe/Pilot/Scale production budgets.

### Bryan Guerra — AI dropshipping automation

Material:
- `How to Start Dropshipping with AI in 2027 (Step by Step CreateYourStore AI Tutorial)`

Durable learning:
- store creation/import/inventory/pricing automation reduces operations cost;
- none of it creates demand by itself.

Impact: **REJECTS** store-first architecture; **AUTOMATES** storefront/ops adapters.

### Koda — China sourcing/import sites

Material:
- `5 Melhores Sites para Importar Direto da China em 2026`

Mentioned surfaces:
- AliExpress;
- Shein;
- Taobao;
- Xianyu/Goofish;
- 1688;
- CSSBuy-style purchasing/consolidation agent.

Durable learning:
- deeper China sourcing can reduce product cost;
- wholesale/marketplace/used-goods sources have different identity/quality/MOQ risk;
- purchasing agents can provide QC/consolidation and logistics access.

Caution:
- landed cost, duties, declaration, authenticity and fulfillment must be independently verified;
- lower source price does not imply profitable resale.

Impact: **CHEAPENS** possible Commerce sourcing but only after underwriter/identity gates.

### Honey Hammer — first real zero-inventory resale test

Internal project evidence, not guru material.

Durable learning:
- demand keyword != exact SKU;
- `EXACT / VARIANT / DIFFERENT / UNKNOWN` identity classification is required;
- repeated polling of a weekly trend snapshot does not produce velocity information;
- supplier cost and retail-reference price are separate facts;
- seller account health can make an opportunity non-executable;
- gross spread is not contribution;
- supplier reliability, working capital and payout lock are economic constraints;
- first implementation needs Supplier/SupplierOffer, underwriter, account-health, listing/order/fulfillment/settlement.

Impact: **CONFIRMS / MODIFIES** Commerce Engine with the strongest available internal operational evidence.

## I. Verification policy

Before operationalizing a creator claim, classify it:

```text
source claim
→ current official docs if platform fact
→ independent market/economic evidence if return claim
→ Business Master PROBE if still uncertain
```

Never convert:
- revenue screenshot;
- viral-view anecdote;
- creator RPM claim;
- ad longevity;
- one successful store/channel;

into an assumed expected return without our own evidence.
